Showroom hours and location

S.M. Autos is open Monday to Saturday, 11:00 AM to 10:30 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays. Walk-ins are welcome, but booking a test ride in advance through the website guarantees a bike will be ready for you.

Test rides

Test rides are free and available for any in-stock model. Riders must be 18 or older, bring a valid motorcycle license, and leave a copy of their CNIC at the front desk for the duration of the ride. Test rides are limited to a 15-minute loop around the showroom's approved route. Helmets are provided.

Financing and installment plans

S.M. Autos partners with local banks to offer installment plans on all models over PKR 300,000. Typical plans run 12, 24, or 36 months with a minimum 20% down payment. Approval usually takes 2–3 business days and requires a salary slip or business proof of income, CNIC copy, and two references. Electric bikes currently do not qualify for the extended 36-month plan.

Warranty

Every new motorcycle sold by S.M. Autos includes a 2-year or 20,000 km manufacturer warranty, whichever comes first. The warranty covers engine and transmission defects but does not cover normal wear items such as brake pads, tires, chains, and consumables. Electric models carry a separate 3-year warranty on the battery pack specifically.

Free services and maintenance

New bikes come with 3 free service visits at 500 km, 2,500 km, and 5,000 km. These cover oil changes, chain adjustment, and a general inspection. Parts replaced during a free service (beyond routine consumables) are billed at cost. After the third free service, all maintenance is billed at standard workshop rates.

Trade-ins

We accept used motorcycles in running condition as partial payment toward a new bike. Trade-in value is assessed in person based on mileage, condition, and documentation (original registration and CNIC matching the owner). We do not accept trade-ins for bikes reported stolen or with outstanding loans against them.

Delivery and registration

In-city delivery is free. Out-of-city delivery is charged based on distance, quoted at the time of purchase. S.M. Autos handles registration paperwork with the local motor vehicle registration authority for an additional service fee, or customers may handle registration themselves at no extra charge.

Return policy

Motorcycles may be returned within 3 days of purchase only if they have not been registered and have fewer than 50 km on the odometer. A 5% restocking fee applies. Custom-ordered colors or accessories are non-refundable.

Contact

For anything not covered here, reach the showroom through the Contact page on the website, or call the front desk during business hours.
